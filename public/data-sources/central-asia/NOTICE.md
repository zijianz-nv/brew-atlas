# Central Asian local brewery products / 中亚地方酒款

Reviewed: 2026-10-02. This addition contains 14 products from 3 breweries in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan. It is a small regional sample, not an award or sales ranking.

Product names, styles and measurements are attributed below. Chinese introductions summarize product-specific facts; BAZA packaging/specification summaries do not imply independently verified tasting notes. Parameters from differing package versions are not combined. Missing information stays null.

Location evidence ties each brewery to a publicly identified locality. Coordinates are city/village references and do not identify surveyed factory buildings. In particular, BAZA is in the Almaty region; the CBG distributor office in Almaty is not the brewery location. SIGMA is associated with Shymkent, not its Almaty bar. Bemer is in Maevka near Bishkek.

Original product packshots are cached unchanged and credited per record. Image SHA-256 and display bounds are retained in the catalogue. Product texts, packaging artwork, photographs and trademarks remain their owners’ rights; no blanket open-content licence is asserted. The Maevka locality coordinate is attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Other locality references are linked below.
