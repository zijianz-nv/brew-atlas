# Active local catalogue — 2026-10-01

The sole active catalogue is `/data/catalog.json`. At the user's request, it now
combines all previously obtained local beer data: the award/representative seed
catalogue, BeerTasting, Open Food Facts, the world reference collection, DIY Dog
historical recipes, Open Beer Database, KKCP, Hold My Beer, Luca, HopCity,
Systembolaget's 2025 beer sales articles, and BeerRepublic product samples.
Awards and representative membership are optional labels, not admission gates.
Records are not all verified independent craft beers or currently sold products.
Exact IDs, reviewed products and barcodes are merged; remaining source records
may describe the same product or different packaging. Source dates, URLs,
licences and historical context remain attached to the data. Systembolaget
sales describe its Swedish channel and year, never a global sales ranking.

Award evidence: https://awards.untappd.com/explore/ and individual region links
retained per record. Annual ratings describe the rating calendar year, not sales,
all-time ratings, ceremony years, or independent craft certification. Unresolved
award IDs and ambiguous Georgia geography remain in the research queue.
This is a partial public-page sample, not an official complete dataset delivery.

Representative seed facts and original packshot URLs are attributed per record.
Existing local source photos are restored as well. Candidate remote image URLs
are not used as live images or claimed to have been downloaded. Missing data
remain null. Coordinates
are publicly sourced city/locality reference coordinates tied to official brewery
address evidence; they are not surveyed production sites for each package.

Public access does not grant an open-content license. Official texts, packaging
artwork, trademarks and photos remain the rights of their owners; no blanket
redistribution/commercial image license is asserted. This local prototype retains
source URLs, retrieval times and image hashes for review. The Chinese short
introductions are faithful paraphrases of official product facts. The full source
evidence is in `research/curated-catalog` and
`research/untappd-awards-2026-09-21` in the source project.

The “global representative 1000” is an editorial target. The initial six seeds
are illustrative, not a completed global selection or sales ranking. Award and
representative memberships may overlap after explicit identity verification.
